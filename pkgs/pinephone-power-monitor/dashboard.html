Battery flow is measured at the RK818 fuel gauge. Positive values mean energy leaving the battery; negative values mean net charging. While plugged in, this is not the phone's total wall-power consumption. Raw samples are retained in /var/lib/pinephone-power-monitor/samples.csv.
